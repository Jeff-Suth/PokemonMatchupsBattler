Every attacking type, ranked

Ranking: most opponents hit super effectively first, then fewest opponents that resist or are immune, then highest total damage multiplier. Levitate is counted: Ground moves have no effect on Pokémon that have it. Other abilities (Flash Fire, Water Absorb, Wonder Guard and so on), held items and weather are not counted.
